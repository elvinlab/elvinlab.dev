# Design

Source of truth for how elvinlab.dev looks and moves (design v3, "lab notebook"). Brand narrative and raw token values live in [BRAND.md](BRAND.md); this file explains how they are applied. The interactive prototype is a private Claude Design canvas; everything needed to build the site is summarized here.

## Direction

- Subject: a portfolio and the blog "Lab Notes — by an eternal junior" of a full-stack engineer who builds with AI agents. Audience: engineers, tech leads and recruiters. Spanish is the default locale at `/`; English is optional under `/en/`. The browser language only triggers a quiet, dismissible line of text ("also available in English") on Spanish pages, never a redirect (SEO and performance) and never on English pages, where the navbar always offers the switch. The English menu says plainly "Notes" even though the notes index is Spanish, and the site notice strip only appears when `notice` is set in the config (it is off now that the site is live).
- Identity comes from the subject: a lab and its notebook. Notes are numbered entries (`Note 003`), each summarized by a **decision record**: context, decision, outcome. On a note page from `lg` (1024 px) up the record sits in the banner to the right of the title (a dashed panel, fields stacked); on phones and in reading mode it stays above the body.
- **One bold element:** the full-bleed banner with a large pixel-face display headline over a static lab-paper background (fine grid, measuring ticks on the left edge, three registration crosses, one ink hue, no glows or stars; `Banner.astro`). The live cursor-reactive WebGL2 effects (galaxy, cursor-waves) are visitor choices in the navbar picker, not the default (`background` is `{ galaxy: false, cursorWaves: false }` in `site.config.ts`). Pink block accents are small and static across the home, navbar, about page, notes index and footer. Everything else stays quiet.
- **The author is in the hero:** from `md` up the home banner shows the drawn avatar with the name and role at the right (14 rem, centered over the sidebar column from `lg`), inside `[data-hero-content]`. The sidebar author card hides its photo from `md` up while the banner is expanded and shows it again when the visitor collapses the banner, so the photo is visible exactly once; each one eases in with `fade-in-up`. Phones keep the photo in the card.
- **Signature, the lab notebook (decided 2026-10-12):** the tagline "I write down what goes wrong" is visible design: a struck-through mistake with its correction (`Fix`), hand-drawn ink underlines and circles (`Ink`), stamps and margin notes. Reason: a review called the site template-like (identical dark cards with a violet glow). Rules in [Notebook ink system](#notebook-ink-system-decided-2026-10-12); brand side in [BRAND.md](BRAND.md#firma-el-cuaderno-de-laboratorio-decidido-2026-10-12). The data-to-screen raw-data block stays on `/me` only. Glow is not part of the evolved identity: the existing violet glow tokens stay until the owner removes them, with no new use.
- **Fewer boxes (decided 2026-10-10):** the home main column (latest note, notebook index, "Now") sits directly on the page background, separated by space and a dashed hairline; the only boxes are the decision record, the three sidebar cards and the newsletter band. The `/notes/` index follows the same pattern since 2026-10-12: the search field container and the four sidebar blocks (author, categories, tags, RSS) are flat, separated by dashed hairlines, and each row keeps the language badge on the date row. The latest note is still one clickable block (`data-card-link`) and its title turns primary on hover instead of a background change.
- Varied hierarchy instead of identical cards: the latest entry is large, older entries are index rows, experiments are full-width exhibition pieces (`ExperimentPiece`), and (in the `full` preset) the four pillars share one strip.
- Mechanics from [Fuwari](https://github.com/saicaca/fuwari) (MIT): grid, measurements, sidebar, motion timing. Identity is ours.
- White-label: another developer replaces name, bio, notes, experience, colors, favicon and socials through configuration, content and one theme file; the layout, motion and motifs above stay in code.

## Appearance presets

`appearance: 'minimal' | 'full'` in `site.config.ts` picks how loud the site is. `minimal` is the calm look; `full` is the original look and the choice of this site since 2026-10-02 (it ran `minimal` from 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-02). The schema default is `full`, so a fork written before the preset existed does not change; this repo sets `full` explicitly. The preset reaches the document as `html[data-appearance]` (`BaseLayout`) and changes three things, each in one CSS file or resolver so components never test the preset:

- **Type scale:** `apps/web/src/styles/type-scale.css` (see Type).
- **Banner and note layout:** `apps/web/src/styles/calm-layout.css` (banner heights and padding, hero measure, decision record) and `note-meta.ts` (where the language badge and author go).
- **Home sections:** optional `home` booleans (`heroPills`, `authorCard`, `hiringCard`, `now`, `pillars`, `notebookIndex`, `experiments`) override the preset, resolved by `shared/config/appearance.ts`. `minimal` turns off `heroPills` and `pillars`; `full` shows everything (this site turns `heroPills` and `pillars` off through `home` since 2026-10-10) (`now` is on in both presets); `experiments` also needs `features.experiments`. A section that is off renders nothing, and with every sidebar card off the aside column does not render. Reference: [CONFIGURATION.md](CONFIGURATION.en.md#appearance-and-home-sections).

Brand tokens, fonts, colors, motion and the accessibility floor are identical in both presets. Evidence and decisions: [minimal appearance preset](../odd/tasks/minimal-appearance-preset.md).

## Avoid

No ALL-CAPS labels on pages. Two bounded exceptions: the language codes ES/EN in `LangBadge` and the navbar locale switch (codes, not labels), and, since 2026-10-12, the notebook stamp (see the ink system): mono 600, 13 px or more, at most two words and 12 characters, only inside the stamp component. No new glows, no new gradients, no looping decoration (2026-10-12). No meta strings joined with a middle dot (use spacing or separate elements). The one exception is the document title: `<title>`, `og:title` and the image alt text that mirrors it read "Page · elvinlab.dev" with a middle dot (decided 2026-10-11, one place in `Seo.astro`, pinned by `link-previews.spec.ts`); never an em dash as a separator in titles, labels or UI strings. Mono is for entry numbers, dates, counts, tags, code and short labels, never sentences. Pixel is for short, large text (page titles included). No gradient text, no `→` in link text, no stock images.

## Type

IBM Plex Sans (variable, `@fontsource-variable/ibm-plex-sans`, replaced Space Grotesk on 2026-10-11) for body, UI and note titles, JetBrains Mono for entry numbers and code, all self-hosted via Fontsource. Two pixel faces, each with one job:

- **Pixelify Sans** (`@fontsource-variable/pixelify-sans`, one variable file, weight 600) is the display face for the home hero headline, the page titles of the inner pages (`PageHeader`: Experiments, Contact, Changelog; and the `/notes/` banner H1) and the accent-bar section titles (`SectionHeading`, so `/me` titles too). It is the core font token `pixel` (`font-pixel`, `packages/core/src/tokens/tokens.json`), so a theme can replace it. Only the Latin file is preloaded (~12 KB), on every page whose H1 uses it (home, `/notes/`, `/experiments/`, `/contact/`, `/changelog/` and their `/en/` twins) through a `<link slot="head" rel="preload">`, never on pages that do not use it. Hero, page and section titles read the preset scale below (`text-hero`, `text-page-title`, `text-section`), with `text-balance` on the hero; the hero measure is a preset variable (16 ch on phones and 24 ch from `md` in `full`, so the full hero sits on two lines on desktop; 22 ch from `md` in `minimal`, so the minimal hero sits on two lines on desktop).
- **Press Start 2P** stays the brand mark: navbar wordmark, retro footer signature and a larger signature on the 404 page.

Rule: pixel faces are for short, large text only, never paragraphs, note prose, note titles, navigation or forms. Known caveat: the pixel `e` is less legible at large sizes; it was judged acceptable at the `minimal` hero sizes (32 / 44 px). Brand decision in [BRAND.md](BRAND.md#tipografía).

### Page header

`shared/ui/PageHeader.astro` is the one header of the inner pages: a pixel `<h1>` (`font-pixel text-page-title`, balanced), an optional description in the body face (`text-text-secondary`, 60ch, `text-pretty`) and an optional `icon` slot for the pixel icons of Experiments and Changelog. It sets the air: about 32 px above on phones and 40 px from `md`, 32 px below. Titles are one or two words. Contact renders it as a full-width row above its two columns so the form and the side panel start at the same top. **Since 2026-10-11 it renders inside the compact lab-paper banner** (the existing `Banner`, not expandable, no WebGL canvas, passed through the `banner` slot as `<PageHeader slot="banner" ...>`), so Experiments, Contact, Changelog, Privacy, Terms, the Subscribe landing and its confirm and unsubscribe pages and the 404 all open with the same paper as the home, `/me`, `/notes/` and the notes. The expand/collapse button and its script moved to `BannerToggle.astro`, filled only by the home, so banners that never expand ship no toggle script.

### Scale

The scale is driven by `html[data-appearance]` and lives once in `apps/web/src/styles/type-scale.css` (web app, not `core`: it is keyed by the app-level `appearance`, while core tokens are keyed by color theme). Components use the `text-hero`, `text-page-title`, `text-section`, `text-note-title`, `text-card-title` and `text-intro` utilities, and `.prose` reads `--type-prose*`; none of them test the preset. Values are rem, so the visitor's font-size setting still scales them. Sizes are phone / from `md` (48 rem):

| Element | `minimal` | `full` (balanced since 2026-10-02) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero (pixel face) | 32 / 44 px | 32 / 52 px |
| Page titles (pixel face, `PageHeader`) | 26 / 32 px | 28 / 36 px |
| Section titles (pixel face) | 20 px | 22 px |
| Note title | 30 / 40 px | 30 / 40 px |
| Latest-note card title | 20 / 22 px | 24 / 30 px |
| Home intro | 17 / 19 px | 17 / 19 px |
| Prose body | 17 px | 17 px |
| Prose h2 / h3 / blockquote | 24 / 19.2 / 17.6 px | 24 / 20 / 18 px |

`full` was the look before the preset existed (hero 36 / 60, section 24, note title 36 / 48, card title 30 / 36, prose 18). On 2026-10-02 the owner approved a smaller scale after measuring the real pages: headings wrapped to 4 or 5 lines on a phone, and long paragraphs ended with one-word lines. The same pass added `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on paragraphs (`global.css`), so even with equal sizes the lines break more evenly. A fork that wants the old look edits the `full` block of `type-scale.css`. On `/me` the at-a-glance values use 20 / 24 px with balanced wrapping, and the availability fact in the sidebar breaks at ` · ` into one line per part instead of cutting a sentence.

Shared by both presets: prose line height 1.75 inside a 68 ch measure (16 px minimum on mobile), small card titles 18 px, meta 13 px (mono meta and chips use the 13 px `text-meta` token; the retro wordmark and signature stay 12 px). Reading mode keeps its own scale (19 px / 1.8, headings pinned to the `full` values). Not on the scale: the `/me` hero, the notes index h1 and the contact, legal and 404 headings. An earlier version of this file gave the `full` note title as 54 px; the real value was 36 / 48 px.

## Layout

- Page width 1200 px; grid `main | sidebar 280 px`, gap 16 px. The sidebar sits on the right and moves below the content under 1024 px.
- Navbar: 72 px card, radius `0 0 16px 16px`, sticky. Items: Home, Notes, Experiments, About (`/me`), Contact. It hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up; hiding closes an open mobile menu, and focus entering the navbar (or keyboard focus already inside it) keeps it on screen, so no control is focused off screen.
- The dismissible locale suggestion stays in document flow after the footer, within the page gutters; it appears only for a browser-language mismatch and never covers content or focus. It can extend document height, but avoids an overlay and does not shift the content above it.
- Banner: every banner takes `min(requested height, preset cap)` through variables in `calm-layout.css`, so a page never asks for a preset. `full` caps are no-ops, so its heights are the requested ones: home 150 px collapsed, `clamp(440px, 56vh, 520px)` expanded (~500 px on desktop), 29 rem on touch or phone-width screens (so the latest note starts near the fold at 390 px), ~340–380 px on the other banner pages. `minimal` caps: page banners 240 px, expanded home `clamp(360px, 48vh, 460px)`, compact 24 rem; banner padding is 5.5 rem top / 1.5 rem bottom (6 rem / 2 rem in `full`). Content overlaps the banner by 56 px.
- Banner edge: every banner blends its bottom edge into the page with the same server-rendered 120 px gradient (`data-banner-fade`, CSS only, no script, so the first paint is right). Before this, only the home had it (`homeFade`) and the notes index, notes and `/me` showed a hard step, worst in light theme. Reading mode hides it with the other banner effects. Pages without a banner: `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/changelog` and the 404.
- Cards: radius 16 px, no borders, no shadows; dashed dividers only. Inner radius follows the concentric rule (outer 16 − padding 12 = 4 px).
- Note cards (the notes index rows and the home latest-note card) are whole-card links: the title stays the only link to the note and its `::after` stretches over the card (no script); the category and tag chips of an index row are separate links to the filtered index (`/notes/?tag=...`) placed above that layer with `relative z-10`, so a click or tap anywhere opens the note, hover tints the card and keyboard focus rings the whole card (`data-card-link` in `global.css`); text inside the card, the decision record included, can no longer be selected by dragging, and the home "Read the note" button is a decorative `aria-hidden` twin of the title link.
- Section titles: sentence case with a 4 × 20 px accent bar.
- Mobile first (390 px reference): single column, sidebar content moves below the main content, notes collapse into one card with dashed dividers.

## Pages

- **Home:** banner with the hero, latest entry with its decision record, notebook index, "Hiring?" recruiter card (availability, CV, link to `/me`), experiments, the dated "Now" card in the sidebar and, depending on the preset, hero pills and pillars. Each section follows its `home` flag (see Appearance presets): `minimal` turns off the hero pills and the pillars; `full` shows all of them. The Now card ("Ahora" / "Now") replaced the old lab log: it lists one to three rows from the `now` block of `site.config.ts` (a mono kind label above the text, dashed dividers between rows, an optional link) under a muted "Updated <date>" line, and renders nothing when the config has no `now` block, so no heading or empty space is left. It is the single dated section allowed by the voice rule in BRAND.md.
- **Subscribe (`/subscribe/`, `/en/subscribe/`):** the page to share, under the compact paper banner (the pixel envelope was dropped on 2026-10-11 because the banner is the accent): a short headline in the pixel display face inside the banner (no eyebrow), the footer's form in a dashed card, three value points and the latest note in the page language. Indexable; the footer band is not rendered on it. Spec in `docs/BRAND.md`.
- **/notes:** Spanish index with search, language filter, entries grouped by year, categories, tags and RSS. English notes have `/en/notes/<slug>` detail routes; there is no English index.
- **Note:** reading-progress bar, decision record before the text (in `minimal` it is tightened: padding 20 to 16 px, gap 20 to 14 px, labels 13 px, body stays 14 px, same headings and content), a header with date and reading time only in `minimal` (the language badge moves to the foot beside the tags, and the author name to the foot meta line; on index rows the badge sits in the chip line and on the home card beside the CTA; `full` keeps them in the header), heading anchors, "Lab note" callout, copyable code (on touch screens the copy button is a 44 px target in its own strip above the first line, so it never covers code; with a mouse it stays the hover-only upstream button), translations (a note and its translation, linked with `translationOf`, declare each other with `hreflang` and `x-default`, and the language switch lands on the translation; invalid links fail the build), prev/next (with its own breathing room, 32 px below the article and 40 px in reading mode), related notes, and sticky table of contents with progress. Discussion shows Giscus comments and reactions only when `features.comments` is on and `site.config.ts` has a `giscus` block (repo and category ids from giscus.app); otherwise the section is absent, with no placeholder. It stays idle until it is within 200 px of the viewport, then loads giscus in the page locale with the built-in light or dark theme, and follows the theme toggle. Fallbacks: a static loading line, an error line, and a `noscript` link to the repository's discussions. Evidence: `tests/browser/comments.spec.ts` (giscus origin stubbed) and the white-label build, which asserts no section without config. **Reading mode** (`features.readingMode`, a central switch; off renders no toggle, script, CSS or stored key): each note has a toggle and, while on, a floating exit button. It sets `html[data-reading]` before first paint from `localStorage` (`reading-mode`) and, with CSS only, hides the site notice, the banner effects and the WebGL canvas (the effect is stopped, the saved background choice is untouched), turns the banner into a compact header, hides the sidebar, flattens the article card and sets one calm column (760 px minus 20 px gutters, so wider than the page measure on phones and narrower on desktop) at 19 px / 1.8. Title, decision record, comments, prev/next, the progress bar and the navbar stay. The served HTML is identical for everyone, so crawlers (no stored preference) see the normal page and SEO is unaffected; on a 390 px phone the text starts at 380 px instead of 538 px. Evidence: `tests/browser/reading-mode.spec.ts` (ES/EN, three widths, axe in both themes), `reading-mode.test.ts` and the white-label build, which asserts nothing of it ships with the flag off. Long-form policy prose uses a 68 ch measure without narrowing the page/sidebar grid.
- **Footprints (`marks`):** a calm, inline pill with a pixel footprint, in the note header (first screen, under the date and reading time) and again at the end of the article; one shared counter. Pink border and icon with the text color (white on pink fails AA), 44 px target, a one-time pink nudge while nobody has left a mark, and an animation picked from the config (`stamp`, `burst`, `pulse`, `none`) that stops under reduced motion. Never floating. The count appears from `marks.showCountFrom` footprints. See ADR 0013.
- **Info tips (`InfoTip`):** the /me right column (quick facts, stack groups, contact card) gives each item an icon and a one-sentence explanation that opens on hover, keyboard focus and tap, in the marks tooltip style (`role="tooltip"`, `aria-describedby`, card surface, divider border, inner radius, mono 13 px). CSS-only plus a tiny script for Escape (WCAG 1.4.13: dismissible, hoverable, persistent); no transition under reduced motion; never printed; anchored to the trigger's left edge and capped at the viewport minus 4.5 rem so it never overflows at 360 px. Below `lg` the quick facts sit under the /me hero (`QuickFacts`, one source rendered twice, the unused copy `display: none`). Stack group hints and icons come from the config (`me.stack[].hint`, `.icon`); other copy lives under the `me.tip.*` i18n keys.
- **Link previews:** every page emits Open Graph and Twitter tags (`summary_large_image`, 1200 x 630, alt text, `og:locale` as `es_ES`/`en_US`). Pages share `/og-image.png` except notes, which are `og:type=article` (published/modified time, tags) and get their own card at `/og/notes/<slug>.png`: dark page, cyan and pink glows, JetBrains Mono, domain in cyan, `NOTA 001` in pink, the title and author with date. Cards are rendered at build time by the `og-images` integration (satori to SVG, sharp to PNG) from the notes on disk, so they exist in builds, not in `astro dev`. `/me`, the portfolio page, has a card per locale at `/og/me-<locale>.png`: the portrait (`identity.photo`, else the avatar) ringed in the brand gradient, name, role, location, years of experience and the availability pill (green dot when `recruiter.openToWork`, the danger color otherwise), all read from the site config; it also has a real title (the owner's name) and the bio as description. The `BlogPosting` structured data carries `image` and `author.url`. Evidence: `tests/browser/link-previews.spec.ts` and `src/integrations/og-*.test.ts`. Social platforms cache previews; refresh them with the Facebook Sharing Debugger or LinkedIn Post Inspector after a change.
- **Experiments page (`/experiments/`, `ExperimentsPage` + `ExperimentPiece` + `ExperimentMedia`):** an exhibition made calm on purpose (task X8b; the owner found the 30-entry page too busy, so every ornament that carried no information was removed). The rules below are final, each with its reason; when two conflict the order is SEO and semantics, then performance, then accessibility, then beauty. **Header:** the page title in display type after a pixel flask (9 x 9 grid, crisp edges, pink: the header's single accent) and the intro line, no rule under it. The decorative `// word` comment stack beside the title renders only when `experiments.words` is configured: it told the visitor nothing and cost bytes on every page. **Big pieces:** each is a full-width piece, a `bg-card` text panel with no outline next to the media frame. From `lg` the two sit in columns (media 58 %, text 42 %) and the media side alternates per piece; below `lg` the media comes first. An entry without an image has **no media column**: it is a single-column panel (max 52 rem), never a fake image, because a placeholder pretends to be content. Pieces are separated by a plain 1 px hairline and generous space, not by a labelled dashed divider (the `02 / 03` label repeated the order the page already shows). The featured (first) piece keeps a very soft violet and cyan radial glow behind the media (masked, no blur filter, one paint); the dither over the glow is gone, it only added noise. Text panel: status pill (square 8 x 8 dot: green running, violet shipped, muted archived), title (display), muted subtitle, three facts under plain mono labels (Problem, My part, Result; the icons are gone, the labels already say it), up to three mono chips and the actions: a primary button to the case (internal; names the note's language when it differs from the page's) with a 9 x 9 pixel arrow, then text links "View site" and "Code" (dashed underline, 44 px targets). The oversized outlined index (`01`, `02`) is gone: decoration without information. Each piece keeps its entry id as an anchor (`scroll-mt-24`), which the `/me` rows link to. `/me` does not use any of this: it shows compact rows (`ExperimentRow`, below) to stay inside the LCP byte budget. All styling is local to these components (scoped, or `xc-` prefixed for the compact card), so none of it reaches the global stylesheet or `/me`. The components live in `src/experiments-routes/`, not in `features/portfolio/`, on purpose: `/me` imports the portfolio barrel (`ExperimentRow`) and Astro bundles the CSS of everything a barrel re-exports, so a page component exported from it would put about 14 KB of CSS on `/me`.
- **Media frame and gallery (`ExperimentMedia`):** one 16:10 box, rounded like cards, hairline outline, `object-cover object-top`, four square-capped pixel brackets (3 px bars, 14 px arms) drawn with a single pseudo-element (CSS gradients, no window controls) and a small muted mono caption bar under the image (12 px): the caption on the left, the counter `1 / 4` on the right (static text inside each slide). Entries with 2 to 4 images are a CSS-only gallery: a `<fieldset>` with a screen-reader legend, one visually hidden but focusable radio per slide, the slides, and a row of small 80 x 50 thumbnail labels (the active one outlined in the primary color, the others dimmed, a cyan ring on keyboard focus, each named "Screenshot 2 of 4: caption"; four fit a 390 px phone without wrapping). The checked radio selects the slide with sibling selectors, so it works without JavaScript, the first slide is visible without it and the arrow keys move between slides natively. Slides that are not selected are `display: none` but stay in the server-rendered HTML, and their lazy images do not download until shown; only the first slide of the first piece is eager (`fetchpriority="high"`). One image is a plain figure. There is no cover for an entry without an image (see the piece above).
- **Experiments motion:** the first piece is static (it holds the LCP element). Later pieces rise in (24 px, transform only: never opacity) with a scroll-driven animation, only under `@supports (animation-timeline: view())` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; without support they are simply there. On hover and keyboard focus the image scales to 1.03 in 400 ms inside its frame and the brackets turn violet; a newly chosen slide dissolves in over about 280 ms in four steps (`steps(4, end)`; the first slide snaps back, so the LCP candidate never fades). Nothing autoplays, loops, blinks or parallaxes, nothing depends on animation to be readable, and every transition and animation is off under reduced motion.
- **Experiments at scale (tiers, sorting, pagination):** the layout is driven by the data only. The big exhibition pieces are the `featured` entries (the build fails with a clear message when more than `experiments.maxFeatured` (3 by default) are featured; with none featured the first entry by the usual sort is the big piece) and they **always lead page 1 whatever the sort**. Every other entry is a compact card (`ExperimentCompact`) in a grid of 1 column on phones, 2 from `sm` and 3 from `lg`, below a "More experiments" `h2` that renders only when that tier is not empty. **Compact card rules (each cuts noise, not information):** a `bg-card` surface with no outline (hover and focus change the background and show a hairline; the focus ring is 2 px primary); no outlined numeral and no corner brackets; no grid placeholder cover: a small 80 x 50 (16:10) cover shows only when the entry has a real image, with explicit width and height (CLS 0), and the card is text-only otherwise; the status pill appears only when the status is not `shipped`, so "Shipped" is not repeated on every card; at most two tags; the description clamped to two lines; a quiet pixel arrow at the end. The card is one link named by its title (`aria-labelledby` on its `h3`), so a screen reader hears the title, not the whole paragraph. **Structure:** the "More experiments" `h2` sits on one row with the sort switch at its right (stacked on a phone) and has no dashed rule; year labels (a plain small mono label, not a heading, so the outline never skips a level: `h1`, `h2`, then the card `h3`) appear only when the visible page holds two or more distinct years and the sort is by date; the listing summary ("Showing 13-24 of 27") and the pager close the list on one quiet row. **Sorting and pagination (the listing kit):** `shared/lib/listing.ts` (pure, tested) sorts, pages, builds the window and the paths; `shared/ui/Pager.astro`, `SortSwitch.astro` and `ListingSummary.astro` render them for any list (education reuses them, see [PORTFOLIO.md](PORTFOLIO.md)). ## Motion

Rewritten on 2026-10-12. The old rule ("stepped only", no page transitions) is replaced because the owner wants fluid, purposeful motion and an app feel; the brand is meant to evolve, so the change is recorded here instead of worked around. Two registers:

- **Pixel register (stepped).** Pixel and retro elements (pixel headline details, the cursor block, the Press Start 2P wordmark, the pixel icons, the experiments gallery dissolve) keep `steps(4, end)` motion.
- **Eased register (everything else).** Named tokens, never a literal curve in a component: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (entrances, ink, transitions), `--ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` (round-trip state changes), and durations 150 ms (feedback), 300 ms (entrance, transitions) and 700 ms (ink drawing). State transitions stay on named properties only, never `all`.
- Rules that stay in both registers: every animation stops or shows its final state under `prefers-reduced-motion`; no JavaScript for motion (30 KiB JS gzip budget); one entrance choreography per page; anything that can be the LCP element animates `transform` only, never opacity; nothing may change layout (CLS); no looping decoration (the existing banner pulses are not extended).
- Onload: `animate-fade-in-up` 300 ms, staggered (navbar 0, sidebar 100, content 150, footer 250 ms). It is the page's one entrance; a page transition must not double it up.
- Press: `scale(0.96)` (`active:scale-96`) with the 150 ms feedback duration.
- Theme switch suppresses transitions for one frame.
- The background picker in the navbar is hidden, with CSS only, under a coarse pointer and under `prefers-reduced-motion`, the two conditions where `BackgroundCanvas` never runs an effect, so it never offers a change the visitor cannot see; a narrow desktop window keeps it because width does not stop the effect.

### Page transitions (allowed since 2026-10-12)

Replaces "Page transitions: none". CSS only, through cross-document `@view-transition { navigation: auto }`; no client router, no View Transitions API script. Hover and keyboard-focus prefetch (Astro `prefetch`, `hover` strategy, tap on slow or data-saver connections) stays and helps them.

- **Fallback:** it must degrade to an ordinary page load. Firefox is treated as unsupported (caniuse lists it as partial and unspecified); navigation stays fully correct without transitions.
- **LCP:** never cross-fade the LCP element. Override the default root cross-fade (`::view-transition-old(root)` and `::view-transition-new(root)`) with a transform or clip animation.
- **Reduced motion:** the global `*` reset does not reach view-transition pseudo-elements, so they get their own `prefers-reduced-motion` rule (`animation: none`).
- **Names:** `view-transition-name` values are unique per page and must not outlive the snapshot, so a bfcache restore never reuses a stale name. How to remove them without JavaScript is an open point for the implementation task (N4); if it cannot be done in CSS, the item stays out of scope rather than adding script weight.
- **No double entrance:** `animate-fade-in-up` must not run on top of a transition; the transition replaces it on navigated loads.
- Last in the rollout order (see the signature plan), because it carries the highest LCP, CLS and double-entrance risk.

## Notebook ink system (decided 2026-10-12)

- **Color:** semantic tokens only; pink is the single accent of a block, primary otherwise; never a raw hex in a component. Explicit exception: a `background-image` SVG cannot read CSS variables, so its per-theme stroke colors are written there and documented in the component.
- **No gradients and no glow** on anything new.
- **Density:** at most one ink effect per screen block and a handful per page; more reads as a template in reverse.
- **Semantics:** ink is decorative and `aria-hidden`. The correction keeps its meaning with `<del>` and `<ins>` plus a visually hidden label localized through i18n (`sr-only`, never hardcoded Spanish).
- **LCP:** ink never changes the text of an LCP element; only the stroke animates (700 ms, `--ease-out`, transform or stroke offset, never opacity of the text).
- **Shapes:** a circle only for short single-line phrases: it is `nowrap` and its SVG stays inside the line box (no vertical overhang), so it cannot touch the line above on a wrapped heading. The underline may wrap (per-line background, `box-decoration-break: clone`).
- **CSS home:** `Fix.astro` and `Ink.astro` carry their own scoped `<style>`; the per-theme stroke colors are `:global([data-theme=...])` rules in the same files (still no `global.css` bytes). Verified in a production build (2026-10-12): the rules are inlined only in pages that render the component (a note with both gained about 3.6 KB of HTML including markup; `/me/` and `/notes/` were byte-identical). In `astro dev` the scoped CSS of components used inside draft MDX did not appear; that is a dev-server quirk, not a production defect. Screen-reader label: `notes.fix.correctedTo`, from `Astro.currentLocale`. Tests: `tests/browser/ink.spec.ts` against the `smoke-es` and `smoke-en` fixture notes.
- **Automatic prose treatment (2026-10-12):** every note gets the ink look from `apps/web/src/features/notes/components/note-prose.css`, with zero edits to its content: a pink mono counter on each `h2` (CSS counters, same set and order as the table of contents, hung in the margin from `md`, above the heading on phones, empty alt text so it stays out of the accessible name), a marker band on `strong` (inset `color-mix` shadow of the pink token, per line), a hand-drawn underline on links in running text (redrawn in 250 ms on hover and focus-visible, static under reduced motion), the `Fix` strike on markdown `del` (`.fix-wrong`/`.fix-right` excluded so nothing is styled twice) and a pink italic `ins`, the blockquote bar as a tilted SVG mask painted with the pink token, and `hr` as a wavy mask line. Only the link underline and the strike need per-theme data-URI strokes (custom properties set once per theme); the rest reads tokens. CSS home: the file is imported with `?inline` and emitted as one `<style>` in the note's head, because a bundled CSS import leaked +4.3 KB into every page that reaches `NotePage` through the feature barrel; it is never in `global.css`. Reading mode keeps the numbers above the heading; print drops the band and the masks for solid rules. Skipped: scroll-driven H2 underline (no clear LCP/CLS gain for the bytes).
- **Stamps:** the bounded all-caps exception in Avoid; text such as FUNCIONÓ, FALLÓ, PENDIENTE comes from i18n.

## Native feel layer (decided 2026-10-12)

The goal is the motion and touch feel of an app, built without JavaScript. The honest limit: on the web this is feel, not native performance.

- `viewport-fit=cover` with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` padding: `body` takes the left and right insets, the navbar the top one, the footer the bottom one (`max(2rem, inset)`) and back-to-top the bottom one (`global.css`, `BackToTop.astro`). Every inset is 0 on ordinary screens, so nothing moves there.
- `overscroll-behavior: contain` on `html` stops scroll chaining and keeps the native bounce.
- `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent` on `html` plus a visible `:active` feedback (`active:scale-96`, a feedback transform that the global reduced-motion block shortens to 0.01 ms) on the navbar links and toggles, footer links, standalone chips, back-to-top and note rows (the whole row presses, so chips inside a row do not add their own); 44 px touch targets kept. Each new utility costs bytes on all pages, so reuse `active:scale-96`.
- `theme-color` follows the in-page theme toggle: the `ThemeToggle` script copies the computed `--color-page` into every `theme-color` meta on load and on each switch. The two `prefers-color-scheme` tags stay as the no-JS fallback. Evidence: `tests/browser/native-feel.spec.ts`.
- Still pending the owner: a web app manifest with `apple-touch-icon`. No service worker (cache risk with `_headers` and prefetch).

## Images of experiments and credentials (policy, 2026-10-06)

Decision record: [ADR 0015, images live in the repository, R2 as the documented way out](adr/0015-images-live-in-the-repository.md). Authoring steps: [PORTFOLIO.md](PORTFOLIO.md).

- **Real only.** Own screenshots, own diagrams drawn from a real flow, and public copies of documents. No stock images, no mockups that pretend to be the product, no fake window controls. An experiment without a real image shows the typographic cover (CSS only, decorative), never a placeholder that looks like evidence.
- **How many.** One to four images per experiment, in `images` (optional for every entry; required in practice for the featured one). The first is the cover: `/me` rows and the share card use only it; big exhibition pieces show all of them as a gallery; compact cards use only the cover.
- **Format.** Source PNG, JPG or WebP, 1600 px wide at most and 500 KB at most. Every image is shown in a 16:10 box (the captures are 1280 x 800), cropped from the top. Astro compiles them to webp at several widths with explicit width and height, so nothing shifts while loading.
- **Where they live.** `apps/web/src/assets/experiments/<slug>/<name>.<ext>`, never `public/`: that way they are optimized and a missing file breaks the build. Alt text (required), caption (optional) and order live in the entry's `images`. Credential documents go in `apps/web/src/assets/education/<id>.<ext>` as public copies (no ID numbers, signatures or QR codes); the originals stay outside the repository.
- **Treatment.** A cover is `object-cover object-top` with the existing hairline outline. A document is `object-contain` on a neutral surface at its own ratio, never cropped or recolored. Alt text says what is shown; a gallery image also has a caption.
- **Loading.** Lazy by default. Only an image that is the first thing in the viewport of its page may load eagerly with high priority. `/me` rows use a 96 px thumbnail derived from the cover (no extra asset), kept under about 15 KB.
- **Privacy checklist before adding one.** No email addresses, tokens, private repositories, account names or customer data; capture the public site in a clean state.
- **Themes.** One dark capture is enough; do not ship light and dark pairs until a case needs them.
- **Page weight rule.** Bytes below the fold are almost free for LCP (M3 added 28 KB of lazy image and cards with the LCP unchanged); bytes in the head and the hero are expensive, about 11 ms per raw KB in the lab, which does not compress. Long lists therefore use `content-visibility: auto` and lazy images, only the first image of a page is eager, and a list is paginated instead of growing one page without bound. Measured on 2026-10-07: on every page the LCP element is text with load delay 0, so the whole LCP is render delay, the time to first paint, and it buckets by the size of the inlined `<head>` CSS (about 8-9 ms per raw KB; `/contact/` with 53 KB of CSS about 2110 ms, `/experiments/` with 70 KB about 2270 ms before this work). `build.inlineStylesheets: 'auto'` (an external, cached stylesheet) was tried and **rejected**: the worst-case LCP of `/experiments/` got worse (2436 against 2273 ms) and the runs split into two modes, so `astro.config.ts` keeps `'always'`. The lever is therefore to **ship component CSS only where the component renders**: emit it as a string from the component that renders (`mark-styles.ts`, `shared/ui/listing-styles.ts`, `experiments-routes/experiment-styles.ts`), prefixed and unscoped (no `data-astro-cid-*` on every selector), never as a scoped `<style>` of a component a page imports for a part it does not show. The result on `/experiments/`: inline CSS 70.5 to 60.8 KB, worst-case LCP about 2270 to about 2130 ms. `pnpm check:page-weight` keeps that from regressing (see [TESTING.md](TESTING.md), "Page weight budget").
