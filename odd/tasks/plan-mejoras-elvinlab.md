## T45 (#69) measurement results (2026-10-05, no code changed)
Method: Playwright Chromium on production, mobile viewport 390x844, CDP throttling (CPU x4, 150 ms latency, 1.6 Mbps), 9 runs per case, LCP from `PerformanceObserver`, compared with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (animations effectively off). Navbar: fixture build of the real config, DOM-cloned `<li>` for the extra links, page overflow and wrap measured at 768, 820, 900 and 1024 px, ES and EN. Single machine, simulated throttling: indicative, not Lighthouse.
- Entrance animations and LCP: `/` 528 ms with animations vs 524 ms without; `/notes/como-construi-este-sitio/` 536 vs 528 ms. The LCP element is the hero or note H1 in both, outside the delayed columns, so the animations do not delay it. `/me/`: 2200 ms vs 2124 ms (min 2188 vs 2108); the LCP element is the avatar `IMG`, which sits in the 150 ms delayed column; the roughly 80 ms gap is consistent with the delay but the wrapper was not verified. The `/me/` LCP is dominated by the image (about 2.1 s even without animations), the slowest page against the 2.5 s budget.
- Why the delay can matter in principle: `animate-fade-in-up` uses fill mode `both`, so an element is at `opacity: 0` during its `animation-delay` (aside 100 ms, main 150 ms, footer 250 ms) and Chrome does not count a transparent element as an LCP candidate. Defined in `styles/global.css` and used in `BaseLayout.astro` and `Footer.astro`. All of it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Navbar fit (production config has 4 links): 4 links fit at 768, 820, 900 and 1024 in both locales. 5 links overflow the page at 768 in ES. 6 links overflow at 768 and 820 in both locales and fit from 900. No link wrapped; header height stayed 72 px. Extra labels were "Aprendizaje" and "Proyectos", not the real future labels.
